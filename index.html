THE REAL BORDER CHALLENGE

Understanding the movement of African freight.

BorderFlow brings shipment records, document checks and border operations into one organised digital workspace. These photographs illustrate the freight and transport environment the platform is designed to support.

01 · BORDER OPERATIONS

Border congestion

Long truck queues can create pressure for clearing agents. BorderFlow helps teams organise shipment references, document checks and recorded progress in one workspace.

02 · BORDER NETWORK

Multiple border posts

Freight teams can record shipments at different border posts and keep their operational information organised instead of relying on scattered records.

03 · SHIPMENT MANAGEMENT

Shipment management

Record truck registrations, driver information, cargo descriptions and shipment references so teams can find important shipment details more easily.

04 · FREIGHT COORDINATION

Freight coordination

A central shipment workspace helps clearing agents and freight operators review recorded information and coordinate their daily work.

05 · CARGO OPERATIONS

Cargo visibility

Record cargo descriptions and connect cargo information to the correct shipment, helping teams keep their operational records organised.

06 · TRANSPORT RECORDS

Transport records

Keep shipment progress visible through recorded statuses such as Arrived at Border, Docs Checked and Cleared to Go.

07 · LOGISTICS NETWORK

Connected logistics

Cross-border freight involves many people and transport locations. BorderFlow is designed to bring important shipment information together.

08 · PORT OPERATIONS

Port and cargo operations

Border crossings form part of a larger international freight journey. BorderFlow provides a place to organise the shipment information associated with that journey.

09 · FREIGHT NETWORK

Freight network

Keep freight details, document records and shipment references organised as cargo moves between locations.

10 · MOVING TRADE FORWARD

Moving trade forward

BorderFlow aims to help freight operators manage cross-border work through organised records, document review and shipment progress information.
